## LinkedIn
We built Akshara for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge: a reading screener for early-grade children in Kannada, Tamil and Hindi, on Gnani Prisma and Timbre.

The useful part is what we measured. Across 90 passages and 156 planted mistakes, Prisma caught skipped words (40/41) and unrelated word swaps (36/36). But a single wrong consonant was caught only 8 times in 39, because the transcriber writes the word the child meant.

We tried a pause-based re-check to fix it. Result: no gain, so we left it out and told the user instead. Every result now carries a note that green does not prove a perfect reading.

All audio is synthetic and the samples are small, and we say so. Method, numbers and code: [link]
