# Surface brief: Akshara web app (child flow first, then teacher)

Scope: the demo path. Child: welcome, choose reader, home, reading, word marks, akshara practice,
results with stars. Teacher: dashboard, review. Other routes inherit the world through tokens and
components. Mode: Operate (child and teacher both complete a task). Device: shared classroom
Android tablet, portrait and landscape; must hold on a 360 px phone.

Memorable moment: a missed word breaks into akshara tiles that land one at a time as Timbre says
each, then close back into the word.

Unresolved: Evon sentence screen waits on Daniel's batch; StoryWeaver covers need a credit line
(title, illustrator, CC BY 4.0) wherever one is shown.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The child climbs their own activity ladder, the way a Nali-Kali or ABL classroom works:
one rung at a time, every activity a colour-coded card with a picture symbol. Refuses the
learning-app default of white rounded cards, a mascot and a green progress bar.

OWN-WORLD: Laminated activity cards on a cool limewash ground (#EEF3F7, never cream). One
full-strength hue per activity, one hue field per screen: peacock blue #0B6FA4 reading, marigold
#F6A800 practice, parrot green #2E9E44 rewards, indigo #1B1F4B teacher and ink; kumkum red
#D7263D only for a skipped word. Cards: white laminate, 20 px radius, a thick top band in the
activity hue, a round symbol badge. Baloo 2 family (Baloo 2, Baloo Tamma 2, Baloo Thambi 2) for
all type, so three scripts read as one voice. Real StoryWeaver cover art on every story.

STORY: The child sees where they are on the ladder (ASER rungs: letters, words, paragraph,
story), taps one big card to read, sees each word marked with its own shape, practises the
missed word akshara by akshara, and earns a star on the rung. The teacher sees the class as the
wall achievement chart and settles the doubtful marks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Child home, tablet. Top strip: reader chip (avatar, nickname) left, switch
reader right. Main field: the ladder runs up the left third, four rungs with symbols, the
child's rung lit in peacock. Right two thirds: today's story card, StoryWeaver cover at large
scale, title in the child's script, one peacock Read button (book icon) as the only primary
action. Bottom: parrot-green rewards strip, stars and streak.

FORM: ABL Learning Ladder, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed f04848cf. Raises: syllables
cascade with the voice (split-flap); one hue field per activity (acetate tabs); one thing at full
scale per child screen (catalog sleeve); the word in focus grows (streaming wall); each mark has
its own shape (labanotation); each part shows its length (j-card).
